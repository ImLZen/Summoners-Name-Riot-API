# ADR 0002: Use three resources in v0.01

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-21

## Decision

Use Food, Materials, and Knowledge.

## Rationale

They map directly to growth, construction, and progression. More resources would increase interface and balance cost before proving that eras and respawn are fun.

## Revisit when

Playtests show that trade or geographic specialization lacks meaningful tension that cannot be created through terrain modifiers and structures.
